# Bug Report: Product Filter Button Does Not Work

## 📋 Bug Information
* **Bug ID:** BUG-002
* **Summary Title:** Product filter button is not working
* **Environment:** Google Chrome / Chromebook / Swag Labs (`problem_user`)
* **Severity:** Medium

---

## 🔄 Steps to Reproduce (STR)
1. Log in to the Swag Labs website (`https://www.saucedemo.com`) using `problem_user`.
2. After completing login and entering the main page, click on the product filter button.
3. Select the filter option to sort price from high to low ("Price (high to low)").

---

## ✅ Expected Result
The products should be filtered by type or sorted by price from high to low accurately.

---

## ❌ Actual Result
The filter button/option does not work at all; the product order remains unchanged.
